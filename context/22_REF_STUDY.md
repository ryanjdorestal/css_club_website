## The diagnosis in one paragraph

Run 2 uses Archivo Black (a friendly, round-cornered heavy grotesk) for display and Poppins (a rounded
geometric) for body, and its cards are rectangles with 1px hairlines and a label row. Every reference
Ryan calls "top tier" does the opposite: display faces are **squared, stencil-cut, extended or pixel** —
letterforms built from straight strokes with corners cut away, often paired solid + outline, often cropped
by the frame; body/UI text is **monospace or a squared grotesk**; and the cards are **instruments**, not
containers: chamfered corners, side tabs, registration marks, ID codes, coordinate readouts, segmented
meters, hex hashes, bracketed CTAs, hatch fills, tick rulers. The look is ~70% type + micro-labels and ~30%
layout. Run 2 fixed the 30%.

## Vocabulary, ranked by how often it recurs across the 533 images

1. **Mono caps micro-labels everywhere** — 10–11px, tracking .12–.16em, 50–60% opacity, with a prefix glyph: `/01`, `//SCN_01`, `_status`, `> RENDERING`, `● LIVE`, `[1]`, `↳`. Label sits *above* its value; value is 2–3× larger, tabular, slashed zero.
2. **Hairlines, not boxes.** 1px at 12–18% alpha; zero radius (2px max); grids built with `gap:1px; background:line-color` so dividers are shared; cards tone-on-tone (3% lighter than the band), never shadowed.
3. **Corner brackets / registration marks / tick rulers.** Four 10–14px L-shapes at corners of frames and photos; `+` marks at section corners; tick rulers along an edge; perforation (dashed) along a stub.
4. **ID codes and readouts as decoration.** `TX-4821-HX`, `PL-HV-119`, `K5IC-B12`, `0xFE05A1`, `35.6762° N`, `VERSION 2.0.0-RC1`, `104 : 0768`, `SYS.TIME 23:47:12`. Always real or derived (git SHA, build time, data counts, real coordinates), never lorem.
5. **Chamfered / notched corners and stubs.** One 45° cut (12–24px) on a card or panel, often with a 2px accent bar in the notch; side tabs; speech-stub triangles; buttons with an attached square end-cap holding `↗` or `+`.
6. **Squared / stencil / extended / pixel display type.** Straight-stroke letterforms; bars removed; solid word + outline word on one line (`UTOPIA` + `TOKYO`); half-solid/half-outline single word (`VIT` + `ALITY`); giant type cropped by the container edge; type drawn through by a 1px wireframe or occluded by a 3D object; glyph substitutions (barless A, `1` for I, slashed/dotted 0, `⁄` as a logotype element).
7. **Segmented meters and block progress.** `▮▮▮▮▯▯` or `repeating-linear-gradient` 6px on / 2px off, with a mono % readout; half-arc gauges; tick-mark rings.
8. **Status chips with a dot + word.** `● LIVE`, `● Warning`, `Not Connected`; 6px dot with a 2px halo, 1px border in the same color at 40%, zero radius, tracked caps; LIVE pulses.
9. **Textures at 4–10%:** dot grid (24px), hairline grid, contour/topographic lines, halftone/dither raster (masked to a corner), scanlines, code-rain / glyph walls, wireframe meshes and perspective grid floors, hatch fills (45°, 1px/5px) for "empty" or "glazing".
10. **Sigils.** A geometric mark built on a square grid (Kufic-style wordmark, pixel eye, lambda, four-point star, arrow-in-square); a small pictogram set with 1px square-capped strokes; used as bullets, section marks, watermarks, hover states, and the logotype itself.
11. **Rails at the edges.** Vertical rotated text (`writing-mode: vertical-rl`) with dates/binary/section slugs; numbered nav (`01 Introduction`); vertical index letters `A B C D` with a ring on the active one; right-edge tool column; bottom-of-page status ticker split into cells.
12. **Bracketed / underscored CTAs.** `[ USE SAFE MODE ]`, `>_EXECUTE_OPERATION`, `LET'S BUILD ↗`, `INIT.SEQUENCE`; hard-edged, mono or squared caps, arrow glyph, filled block or 1px outline, never a pill.

## What Ryan's own rhecwb footer does (the bar he set)

Tracked-caps mono column heads (`CONTACT / MENU / CLUB`), body links in a squared grotesk, three
1px-ring icon buttons, a hairline, a mono tagline rail `RHEC · LAGCC · A LEGACY ENGINEERING CLUB`, a
flat red CTA with `→`, a mono legal rail, then the giant cropped wordmark in a heavy squared grotesk at ~6%.
Note the type: it is *not* Archivo Black — it is a squared, slightly extended grotesk with flat curves.
